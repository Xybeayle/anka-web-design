Our story

It started in 2020, when we noticed that hospitality brands deserved the same level of design craft as luxury fashion – but were often overlooked.

We built ANKA from the ground up, combining Alina’s design intuition with Marcus’s development expertise. Today we’re a remote‑first team, but our roots remain in Phuket, where every project begins with a conversation and a cup of coffee. We don’t do templates – we build tailored digital atmospheres that feel like the places they represent.
